About

Practical experience.
Direct advice.

I'm Chris Zavala — an IT professional based in Colorado with over a decade of hands-on experience across infrastructure, workplace technology, and organizational IT strategy. I've worked inside organizations ranging from lean 10-person SMBs to enterprise environments with 1,200+ employees, which means I understand both the budget constraints and the political realities.

My background is practical, not theoretical. I've built and maintained the infrastructure, argued for the tooling budget, and managed the conversation with leadership when the technology isn't working the way anyone promised it would. I write about technology and workplace dynamics at blog.chriszavala.com — the same thinking I bring to client engagements.

I take a limited number of engagements at a time so I can give each one real attention. If you need someone who will give you a straight answer and stick around to help implement it, let's talk.
